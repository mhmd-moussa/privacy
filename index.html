Information that stays on your phone

Game progress, settings, scores and similar data are saved only on your device. Device details that the utility apps show you (Android version, model, storage, battery, memory) are read from your phone and displayed to you; they are not sent to us.

Phone Info - Android Version can scan QR codes and barcodes. The camera image is processed on your phone and is not uploaded or stored by us. The camera is used only after you allow it.

Advertising

The apps are free and show ads from Petal Ads (HUAWEI Ads Kit). To show and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may process device information such as the OAID (Open Anonymous Device Identifier), device model, operating system version, language, network type and approximate region, plus ad interactions such as views and clicks. This processing is done by Huawei under its own privacy terms.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland receive only non-personalized ads. On a HUAWEI phone you can turn off personalized ads or reset your OAID in Settings under Privacy, then Ads and privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Camera (Phone Info only): to scan QR codes, after you allow it.
  • Notifications (some games): for an optional daily reminder that you can turn off.

Children

The apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with anyone except the ad processing described above.
